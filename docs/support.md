# LittleChef Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email **the.cedster@gmail.com**, or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/cedricnagata/little-chef/issues).

## Getting started

- **Download a model for on-device AI.** Go to **Settings → AI Provider** and tap **Download**. The size is shown first (about 3.6 GB for Bonsai 2 9B on most recent iPhones), with a warning on cellular. After the download, the assistant works without internet.
- **Or use a Mac instead.** Install [BigBro](https://github.com/cedricnagata/bigbro/releases) on a Mac with Apple Silicon (macOS 14 or later), choose BigBro in **Settings → AI Provider**, and approve the pairing on the Mac. The Mac and iPhone must be on the same network. Nothing is downloaded to the iPhone.
- **Without either,** recipe websites that publish structured recipe data still import, and timers can be set by hand.

## Common questions

**Which model does my iPhone get?** It depends on how much memory iOS lets the app use: the largest of Bonsai 2 9B, 4B and 2B that fits, and Bonsai 2 27B as an option on devices with plenty (such as iPads with 16 GB). Only Bonsai 2 9B and 27B (or a Mac through BigBro) can set timers and change recipes for you; the smaller models answer questions.

**Voice isn't working.** LittleChef asks for the microphone and speech recognition the first time you use voice. If you declined, turn them on in iOS **Settings → LittleChef**.

**How do I move my recipes to another device?** Everything is kept on the device, with no syncing. On the old device, go to **Settings → Export Profile** and save the file to Files or AirDrop it. On the new one, **Settings → Import Profile** and choose the file. It brings your recipes, inventory, shopping list and settings, and adds them to anything already there. Export a profile before deleting the app, too, if you want to keep your recipes.

**The Mac doesn't show up.** BigBro must be running, and macOS must have been allowed Local Network access for it (asked on first launch). On the iPhone, check **Settings → LittleChef → Local Network**.

**How do I delete my data?** **Settings → Delete All Data** in the app. It removes your recipes, inventory, shopping list, settings, downloaded models and paired Macs from the device. It can't be undone.
